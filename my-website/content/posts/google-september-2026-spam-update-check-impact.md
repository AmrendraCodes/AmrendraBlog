Google launched the September 2026 spam update on September 24 at 9:15 a.m. PDT. The update is global and multilingual, and it may take up to two weeks. To evaluate the impact of the spam update, compare the number of clicks in your Search Console before and after September 24 and eliminate [technical issues](/services/web-development) and manual actions.

## Key Highlights

- This is Google&#x27;s fourth spam update of 2026, after March, June and August.
- Earlier updates finished fast: 19.5 hours, 2 days and 2 days 16 hours.
- Google has not said which spam policy this update targets.
- Rankings may keep moving until early October.
- Measure first. Do not make big changes yet.

## How to Check Google Spam Update Impact

### Step 1: Confirm the Timing

Find the date your drop began. If it started before September 24, this update is not the cause.

### Step 2: Use Google Search Console

The Google Search Console spam update check starts in Performance → Search results.

- Turn on the Compare date range.
- Compare the last 7 days with the 7 days before September 24.
- Review clicks, impressions and average position.
- Open the Pages tab and sort by click change.

Search Console recently split web data into text and multimodal filters. Use the same search type on both sides, or your numbers will mislead you.

### Step 3: Find the Pattern

- Whole site down: a sitewide signal or a technical fault.
- One section down: likely a content pattern in that section.
- A few pages down: probably normal movement.

### Step 4: Check Manual Actions

Open Security & Manual Actions in Search Console. A manual action is a separate issue with its own reconsideration process.

### Step 5: Rule Out Other Causes

Check for broken redirects, robots.txt changes, downtime, slow pages and seasonal demand. Our [SEO content strategy service](/services/seo-content-strategy) covers audits like this.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Traffic drops cause panic, and panic causes bad decisions. Watch for these red flags:

- Rewriting everything in week one. The rollout is not finished.
- Deleting pages in bulk. You may remove pages that were never the problem.
- Blaming AI content by default. Google has not confirmed that. Scaled, unhelpful content is the real risk.
- Trusting volatility trackers. They measure their own keyword sets, not your site.
- Buying "recovery" packages or links. This can make things worse.

## Google Spam Update Recovery Steps

If your data shows a real hit after September 24, wait for the rollout to end, then:

1. List affected pages from Search Console with their click loss.
2. Match each page against Google&#x27;s spam policies, honestly.
3. Fix or remove violations, such as thin doorway pages or hidden text.
4. Improve what remains with real experience, original detail and clear author information.
5. Request indexing for key fixed pages, then monitor for 4 to 8 weeks.

Recovery is not always fast. Google may need to re-crawl your site first. If your Next.js pages do not render well for crawlers, our [frontend development guides](/category/frontend-development) can help.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. When did the September 2026 spam update start?

Google launched the update on September 24, 2026, at 9:15 a.m. PDT. It is a global rollout that applies across all languages simultaneously.

### 2. How long will the rollout take to complete?

Google stated the update can take up to two weeks to fully roll out, placing the expected completion window around October 8, 2026.

### 3. Which specific spam policy was targeted in this update?

Google has not specified the exact spam tactic or policy targeted in this release. Any claims pinpointing specific techniques remain unconfirmed speculation.

### 4. How can I check if my site was impacted?

Compare performance metrics in Google Search Console for the seven days following September 24 against the previous week to evaluate changes in clicks and positions.

### 5. Will Search Console show a notification for a spam update?

No, algorithmic spam updates do not generate notifications in Search Console. Messages appear only when a manual action is issued by a human reviewer.

### 6. What is the main difference between a spam update and a core update?

Spam updates specifically target policy-violating tactics like cloaking or hidden text, whereas core updates focus broadly on evaluating overall content quality and relevance.

### 7. Should I immediately rewrite my content if traffic drops?

No, avoid making hasty changes while the update is actively rolling out. Wait until the rollout officially ends to accurately analyze traffic patterns.

### 8. Are AI-generated articles automatically penalized by this update?

Not automatically. Google evaluates content based on usefulness and adherence to spam guidelines rather than whether content was drafted using artificial intelligence tools.

### 9. How do I recover if my site was negatively affected?

Identify affected pages, review them carefully against Google spam guidelines, fix policy violations, improve content quality, and re-request indexing after completion.

### 10. How many spam updates has Google released in 2026?
